# Code of Conduct

Quiet AI is a small community project, and we want it to be a friendly place to ask questions, report problems and contribute. This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## In short

- Be kind and assume good intent. Many people here aren't native English speakers.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- No harassment, insults, discrimination or unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Keep other people's private information private. That includes chat transcripts, file paths and settings they shared to get help.

## Scope

This applies in the issue tracker, pull requests, discussions and anywhere else you represent the project.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour makes you uncomfortable, contact the maintainer privately through [nikitashulga.com](https://nikitashulga.com). Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may edit or remove contributions, or block people who don't follow this code. The [Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines) are used to decide what happens next.
