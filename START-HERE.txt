Quiet AI - let your AI finish, let your computer rest.

INSTALL
  Windows: keep this whole folder somewhere you can write to (for example
           Documents\QuietAI), then open QuietAI.exe.
  Mac:     move Quiet AI.app to Applications, then open it. The first time,
           allow it in System Settings > Privacy & Security > Open Anyway.
  Nothing else needs to be installed.

USE
  1. Open Settings (sliders icon, top right). Codex and Claude are found
     automatically.
  2. Claude Code: click Connect once, then restart open Claude Code sessions.
  3. Choose After AI finishes or On a timer, the action and the timings.
  4. Press Start monitoring (Enter). Esc stops the plan or the countdown.

Keep Quiet AI open while a plan is armed. Updates: Settings > About > Update now.
